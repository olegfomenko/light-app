## Full description

```
LightApp is a non-custodial Bitcoin Lightning wallet built on Blockstream Greenlight. Instead of hiding the network behind a balance and a send button, it gives you a full Core Lightning node — and the controls to actually run it.

Your node runs on Greenlight's infrastructure, but its keys do not. Every signature is produced on your phone by a signer inside the app. Greenlight can schedule and serve your node; it cannot move your funds.


BEFORE YOU INSTALL

LightApp requires your own Blockstream Greenlight developer credentials (a certificate and key) to register or recover a node. Without them the app cannot connect. This is a tool for people running their own Lightning infrastructure, not a custodial account you sign up for.

The wallet operates on Bitcoin mainnet only.


WHAT YOU CAN DO

Lightning
• Create invoices with the full argument set: fixed or any amount, description, expiry, label, CLTV, on-chain fallback address, custom or auto-generated preimage, and private channel exposure
• Pay bolt11 invoices and bolt12 offers with xpay, including maxfee, retry window, max delay, partial amounts and askrene layers
• Watch payments route in the background while you keep using the app
• Review every invoice you issued and every payment you sent, with payment hashes and preimages

Channels and liquidity
• See inbound and outbound liquidity at a glance, per channel and in total
• Open channels by connect string with amount, minimum confirmations, feerate and announce control
• Inspect reserves, fees and HTLC limits, and close channels when you are done

On-chain
• Receive to a fresh bech32 address, generated new after each use
• Send with feerate presets and a send-max option
• Browse your transaction history with confirmations and the inputs and outputs your node tracks
• Coin control: every UTXO, filtered by confirmed, unconfirmed, spent or immature
• Open any transaction or output in a block explorer


SECURITY

• Your 24-word BIP39 recovery phrase and your Greenlight credentials are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, backed by StrongBox where your device provides a secure element
• That key cannot be used while the device is locked
• Seed screens block screenshots and screen recording
• Copying your seed or private key marks the clipboard sensitive and clears it automatically
• The app is excluded from device backups
• No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no servers of ours


OPEN SOURCE

LightApp is MIT licensed and the full source is public. Read it, build it yourself, or verify what it does with your keys before you trust it with anything:

github.com/olegfomenko/light-app


ALPHA SOFTWARE

This wallet handles real bitcoin on mainnet and has not had a third-party security audit. Start with small amounts, write down your recovery phrase, and do not put in more than you can afford to lose.

Requires Android 9 or newer on a 64-bit ARM device.
```
