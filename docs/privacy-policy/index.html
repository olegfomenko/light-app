LightApp

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 August 2026 · applies to the Android app app.light.wallet

The short version

LightApp collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no servers operated by us. Nobody is watching what you do in the app.

Your recovery phrase and credentials are encrypted and stay on your phone. The one party that sees anything is Blockstream Greenlight, which runs your Lightning node — and it can never move your funds, because signing happens on your device.

1. Who is responsible

LightApp is a free, open-source project maintained by an individual developer. There is no company behind it, no backend of ours, and no database anywhere holding information about you.

Because the app collects no personal data, there is no data controller in the usual sense. Where this policy describes information leaving your device, it is going to a third party you are choosing to use — never to us.

2. Data we collect

None. To be specific, LightApp does not collect, transmit or store:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or any account identifiers — the app has no sign-up
  • Analytics, usage statistics, event tracking or performance telemetry
  • Crash reports or diagnostic logs
  • Advertising identifiers; the app contains no ads and no advertising SDKs
  • Contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location data — none of these permissions are requested

The app declares exactly one Android permission: INTERNET, needed to reach your Lightning node.

3. What is stored on your device

Everything sensitive stays local. The following never leaves your phone unless you deliberately export it:

  • Your recovery phrase — the 24-word BIP39 seed that controls your funds
  • Greenlight credentials — the developer certificate and key you upload, and the device credentials issued when your node is registered
  • Preferences — your display unit choice, which is not sensitive and is stored in plain app storage

How it is protected

  • Encrypted with AES-GCM using a key generated inside the Android Keystore, hardware-backed by StrongBox where your device provides a secure element
  • The key cannot be used while your device is locked, and its material never leaves secure hardware
  • The app is excluded from Android cloud and device backups (allowBackup=false), so your seed is never copied off the phone by the system
  • Screens that display your recovery phrase block screenshots and screen recording
  • Copying your seed or private key marks the clipboard entry sensitive and clears it automatically after 60 seconds
  • Release builds emit no logs

Uninstalling LightApp, or using Delete wallet data in Settings, erases all of this from the device. Neither action affects your node, and neither is recoverable without your recovery phrase — keep it written down offline.

LightApp talks to services you choose to use. We receive nothing from any of them.

5. What Greenlight can see

This is the most important thing to understand about using LightApp, so it is stated plainly.

Your Lightning node runs on Blockstream Greenlight's infrastructure. The app connects to it over an authenticated, encrypted connection using the credentials you supply. In the course of running your node, Greenlight can observe:

  • Your node's identity and the fact that it is online
  • Your channels, balances and the payments your node sends, receives and forwards
  • The IP address your phone connects from

What Greenlight cannot do is spend your money. Your keys are never uploaded. Every operation requiring a signature is sent to the signer running inside the app on your phone, which approves it locally. This is the core design of Greenlight and the reason LightApp is non-custodial.

Greenlight's handling of this information is covered by Blockstream's privacy policy. If you are not comfortable with a third party observing your node's activity, this app is not the right tool for you.

6. Bitcoin and Lightning are public networks

Independently of this app: on-chain Bitcoin transactions are recorded permanently on a public blockchain and can be examined by anyone. Lightning payments are more private, but routing nodes necessarily learn about the payments they forward. LightApp cannot change either of these properties, and no privacy policy can.

7. Children

LightApp is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18. We knowingly collect no data from anyone, children included.

8. Your rights

Privacy regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about you, and to object to its processing.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to disclose, correct, export or erase, and no sale or sharing of personal information takes place — LightApp has never sold or shared any, because it has none. Data on your own device is under your sole control and can be erased at any time from Settings or by uninstalling the app.

9. Security disclosure

LightApp handles real funds and has not undergone a third-party security audit. If you find a vulnerability, please report it privately through the repository rather than opening a public issue, so it can be fixed before it is disclosed.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Because the app's source is public, changes to what it actually does are visible in the commit history as well.
